## 3. What this says

1. **No physics-only model meets the targets on the new reference.** The best single model is tix2h_s0 (S 2.35); the
   best family by mean is tix2 (5.49, but one of its three repeats crashed) or tix2h (6.06, three valid). The family
   differences are smaller than the repeat spreads (1.7-9.5). By the ITERML elitism rule, no family beats another.
2. **Ensembling is the biggest single gain.** P0 (the mean of the three ood8 repeats) scores 2.67, against 4.67-14.14 for
   its members. Flame abs dT falls from 23-71 K to 7.8 K and drift is gone (+0.1 K/10 ms). An ensemble of the tix2h or
   tix2 repeats (C4) is the cheapest next step; P0 costs about 2x per step (4.1 vs 2.0 s).
3. **The flame-statistics bounding (tix2/tix2h) cuts OOD on the radiation-cooled gas but not the repeat spread.**
   ood8 flags 27-61 % of advanced cells in the flame phase; tix2/tix2h flag 4.6-27 %. Their mean flame errors are lower
   (27-30 K vs 44 K), but one tix2 repeat is unstable in pure mode. tix2_s1 drove the gas at the nozzle exit below
   300 K from about 107 ms and crashed at 112 ms.
4. **Ignition is the common weak term.** ood8 and tix2 ignite 0.2-1.4 ms early; tix2h scatters around the DI time
   (+0.6, -0.6, +2.8 ms). Only isann, tix1 and tix2_s2 are within 0.2 ms. This is C2's question (coverage vs precision of
   the induction chemistry).
5. **isann_v6** (online-adapted on the OLD reference, labelled isann) transfers almost to target (S 1.16, 2.8 % OOD).
   It is the online-learning benchmark, not a G2 candidate.
6. Low OOD does not mean accuracy: tix1_s0 has 14 % OOD but the second-worst valid flame error (43.8 K).

## 4. Run notes

- **FPE in the shadow-DI check.** GA18 is integrated on a copy of a surrogate-advanced state, which can carry slightly
  negative mass fractions. Since 15:37 the runner retries a crashed leg once with FPE trapping off and notes it in
  `protocol_notes`. Retries: ood8_s1 (ign 4.5 and 5 ms, flame 0.1 s) and tix2_s1 (ign 10 ms, flame 102.5 ms). The other
  10 candidates ran without one. ood8_s1 had failed on the old runner; I reran it with resume (23:14-02:00).
- **tix2_s1** crashed in the flame phase after its FPE retry. That is a genuine divergence of the model (negative T at
  the nozzle exit), so its score is invalid, as the protocol requires. Not retried.
- **OOD counters.** MPI ranks can interleave their counter lines in `log.run`, and a garbled line was read as
  194 million OOD cells (tix2_s2 showed 553 %). `score_protocol.py` now skips inconsistent lines. All flame reports were
  rescored (S unchanged; OOD and cost only).
- Reference: the flame-phase DI snapshots are A1r 0.1-0.15 s. The A1r radiation stall began after about 0.15 s
  (`results/sim1/A1r/G1.md`), so it does not affect this window.
